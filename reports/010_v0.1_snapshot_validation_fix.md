### Boundary inventory

There is one score-versus-breakdown arithmetic consistency check: `RecommendationSnapshot.consistent_evidence` in `backend/app/session_schemas.py`, looping over **all** `evaluation.ranked` entries. The same model is nested in SessionRead, so it applies to creation, persistence validation, finish validation, and saved response readback.

| Evidence role/path | Effect of the original defect |
| --- | --- |
| Selected winner | Can fail if its projected sum differs; selection must also match accepted evidence exactly. |
| Selected near-equivalent alternative | Same arithmetic check, through its ranked entry; selecting an alternative does not avoid it. |
| Other near-equivalent choice | Can block the selected candidate because every ranked entry is checked. |
| Eligible audit candidate outside the choice band | Can block a valid winner, as Cloud Garden does. |
| Unsuitable eligible audit entry | Also checked; there is no suitability-based exemption. |
| Excluded candidate | Unscored and not in ranked; no score/sum check applies. |
| Persisted active/detail/history read and finish | Revalidates nested saved evidence; representation noise can fail readback or finish as well as creation. |

The recommendation projection itself has no additional arithmetic consistency comparison and does not recalculate scores. Other exact comparisons along the boundary check duplicate evidence copies/membership, factor-points copies against breakdown, IDs/titles/context, timestamps, or lifecycle state. They remain exact: those values are copies, not independent arithmetic recomputations. Frozen scorer ranking, suitability thresholds, time-fit boundaries, and near-tie comparisons remain unchanged.

## Root cause

The scorer sums its original seven contributions, which can be a mixture of integer and floating-point values. Pydantic projection converts breakdown values to `float`. On the tested Python 3.12 runtime, summing the all-float projection can differ in the last representation bit from summing the original mixed values. The stored score is the original scorer result, as intended. Exact arithmetic equality at snapshot validation treated representation noise as corrupted evidence.

The defect was in integration validation, not the duration formula, scoring weights, suitability gate, ranking, or recommendation membership. Iron Summit's own total agrees exactly; Cloud Garden's unselected ranked entry was enough to prevent its start.

## Exact production correction

Only `backend/app/session_schemas.py` changes in production. It imports `math.isclose` and `math.isfinite`, defines explicit constants, and replaces the score/sum comparison inside the existing all-ranked loop:

```python
SCORE_REL_TOL = 1e-12
SCORE_ABS_TOL = 1e-12

if not isfinite(item.score) or not isclose(
        item.score, sum(item.breakdown.values()),
        rel_tol=SCORE_REL_TOL, abs_tol=SCORE_ABS_TOL):
    raise ValueError("Snapshot breakdown must sum to score")
```

The finite-score guard ensures infinity is not accepted as equivalent to infinity; a nonfinite breakdown sum also fails comparison against a finite score. Nonfinite values are not legitimate representation noise. The remaining factor-copy, membership, supported-version, timestamp, and session identity checks are untouched.

The invariant remains: **the stored score must numerically agree with the sum of its breakdown**. No score, factor, suitability, ordering, audit entry, or persisted payload is rounded, rewritten, dropped, recomputed, or repaired by validation. Snapshot version remains 1 and policy version remains **v0.1-final-004**. No migrations, dependencies, routes, frontend changes, or broad refactors were needed.

### Tolerance rationale

For finite values a and b, the comparison accepts:

`abs(a - b) <= max(1e-12 * max(abs(a), abs(b)), 1e-12)`.

- Relative tolerance **1e-12** permits approximately **1e-10 points at a 100-point total**, and about **7.04e-11** at Cloud Garden's total. This covers the observed last-bit difference with a small numerical margin while staying far below meaningful score differences.
- Absolute tolerance **1e-12** handles totals at or near zero, where relative tolerance alone would not accommodate cancellation noise. Negative eligible audit totals are supported.
- These are validation precision limits, not tuned scoring thresholds or calibrated confidence. The production score scale and all acceptance/ranking comparisons are untouched.
- Tests accept one-step representational differences, ±`5e-13` near zero, and approximately `5e-11` at 100. Tests reject `2e-12` against zero and approximately `2e-10` at 100, outside the selected tolerances. They also reject both signs of material differences including `1e-6`, `0.01`, and 1 point.
